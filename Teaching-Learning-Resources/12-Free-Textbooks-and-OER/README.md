# Free Textbooks, E-Books and Open Educational Resources

These sites provide free-to-read textbooks, open educational resources, course materials, or public-domain books.

| Resource | Link | Useful For | Access Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenStax | https://openstax.org/ | Peer-reviewed textbooks in mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, statistics, computer science, business, and more | Free online; textbooks are also available as free PDFs |
| CK-12 | https://www.ck12.org/ | K-12 mathematics and science FlexBooks, practice, simulations, and customizable digital textbooks | Free educational content; account may help with some features |
| LibreTexts | https://libretexts.org/ | Open textbooks and learning libraries across STEM, humanities, social sciences, and more | Free online open educational content |
| Open Textbook Library | https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks | Openly licensed college textbooks that can be read, downloaded, and adapted according to license | Free and openly licensed |
| OER Commons | https://www.oercommons.org/ | Open textbooks, lessons, activities, modules, course materials, and teaching resources | Free discovery; individual resource licenses vary |
| MIT OpenCourseWare | https://ocw.mit.edu/ | University course notes, assignments, exams, videos, readings, and some complete textbooks | Free, self-guided access |
| Project Gutenberg | https://www.gutenberg.org/ | Public-domain e-books, classic literature, older science and reference works | Completely free; strongest for older/public-domain works rather than modern school textbooks |
| DIKSHA | https://diksha.gov.in/ | Indian school textbooks, videos, interactive content, courses, quizzes, and state/board resources | Government-supported school-learning platform |
| ePathshala | https://epathshala.nic.in/ | NCERT textbooks and digital school-learning resources | Free access to NCERT digital resources |

## Choosing a Resource

Before using a book or OER in class, check:

- grade level,
- curriculum alignment,
- publication/update date,
- accuracy,
- accessibility,
- reuse licence,
- whether teacher solutions are intended for students.
