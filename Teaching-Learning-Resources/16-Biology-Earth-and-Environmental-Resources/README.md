# Biology, Earth and Environmental Resources

| Resource | Link | Useful For |
|---|---|---|
| HHMI BioInteractive | https://www.biointeractive.org/ | Free biology activities, interactives, videos, animations, virtual labs, real data, genetics, evolution, ecology, cells, and physiology |
| NCBI Bookshelf | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/ | Free online books and reference works in biology, medicine, biotechnology, and health sciences |
| iNaturalist | https://www.inaturalist.org/ | Observing organisms, biodiversity projects, species identification support, and citizen science |
| GBIF | https://www.gbif.org/ | Global biodiversity occurrence data for ecological and geographic investigations |
| NOAA Education | https://www.noaa.gov/education | Ocean, climate, weather, atmosphere, marine science, real-world data, lessons, and educator resources |
| NASA Learning Resources | https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/ | Earth systems, climate, satellites, space science, and STEM activities |
| Smithsonian Learning Lab | https://learninglab.si.edu/ | Digital museum resources, images, texts, videos, science collections, and educator-created learning sets |
| Concord Consortium | https://concord.org/ | Inquiry tools and simulations for genetics, evolution, Earth systems, molecular science, and data-rich STEM learning |

## Investigation Ideas

Students can use these resources to:

- compare biodiversity between locations,
- interpret weather and climate datasets,
- study adaptation and evolution,
- investigate ecosystems,
- use authentic scientific images and records,
- distinguish observation data from explanation.
