# Teaching & Learning Resources

Teaching and learning resources support hands-on, inquiry-based, project-based, evidence-based, accessible, and research-informed education.

This section is now organised as a **large teacher resource library** combining repository materials with external research databases, open textbooks, simulations, lesson banks, data sources, digital tools, citizen science, maps, primary sources, assessment tools, inclusive-teaching guidance, and professional-learning resources.

- 31 ESP32 & IoT project folders
- 32 detailed AI student prompt projects
- Robotics & Physical Computing handbook covering 82 listed components

- lesson-planning, capstone, assessment, portfolio, presentation and workshop frameworks

## Resource Quality Check

Before building a lesson around an external site, verify:

- current availability,
- grade suitability,
- subject accuracy,
- login/account requirements,
- student privacy and age rules,
- school-device/network access,
- accessibility,
- licence/reuse conditions,
- material/tool cost,
- safety requirements,
- what students will actually learn and produce.

> Online services, pricing and access conditions can change. Links in this library should be rechecked when planning a lesson.
