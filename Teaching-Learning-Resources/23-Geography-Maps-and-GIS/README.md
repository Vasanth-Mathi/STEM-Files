# Geography, Maps and GIS Resources

| Resource | Link | Useful For | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Earth Education | https://www.google.com/earth/education/ | Geospatial thinking, maps, 3D terrain, storytelling, geography, science, history | Free; school Google policies apply |
| Google Earth Education Resources | https://www.google.com/earth/education/resources/ | Classroom activities, lesson ideas, Voyager, geospatial projects | Free |
| National Geographic Education | https://education.nationalgeographic.org/ | Geography, physical/human systems, environment, maps, culture | Free |
| ArcGIS Learn / Education | https://learn.arcgis.com/ | GIS tutorials, mapping, spatial analysis, storytelling | Some free learning; product access varies |
| ArcGIS StoryMaps | https://storymaps.arcgis.com/ | Map-based storytelling and geographic presentations | Account/features vary |
| OpenStreetMap | https://www.openstreetmap.org/ | Open collaborative maps, local mapping, geography projects | Free/open data |
| NASA Worldview | https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/ | Satellite imagery, fires, storms, aerosols, Earth observations | Free |
| USGS Education | https://www.usgs.gov/education | Geology, earthquakes, water, maps, land, Earth science | Free |
| Natural Earth Data | https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ | Free public-domain map datasets for cartography and GIS | Free/public domain |
| geoBoundaries | https://www.geoboundaries.org/ | Open administrative boundary datasets | Free/open |
| MapChart | https://www.mapchart.net/ | Simple thematic maps for classroom visualisation | Free basic use |
| Gapminder Tools | https://www.gapminder.org/tools/ | Animated global statistics linked to geography and development | Free |

## Student Mapping Tasks

- compare physical and human geography,
- map field observations,
- analyse distance and scale,
- overlay environmental data,
- build evidence-based map stories.
