# Student Portfolio and Reflection

## Purpose
Help students document what they personally learned even when projects are completed in teams.

## Suggested Portfolio Sections

1. **Project title and purpose**
2. **What I already knew**
3. **New concepts**
4. **My design / plan**
5. **Circuit, blocks, code, model, or process**
6. **My personal contribution**
7. **Testing evidence**
8. **Problem I faced**
9. **How I investigated it**
10. **Version 1 and Version 2**
11. **Final result**
12. **Remaining limitation**
13. **Sources / acknowledgements**
14. **Reflection**

## Short Reflection Questions
- What was the most important thing I learned?
- What did I misunderstand at first?
- What failed during the project?
- What evidence helped me improve it?
- What did I personally contribute?
- What would I do differently next time?
- Can I explain the project without reading the code or notes?

## Individual Accountability
Team projects should still include individual evidence. Each learner should be able to explain at least one meaningful design, coding, testing, research, or troubleshooting decision.
