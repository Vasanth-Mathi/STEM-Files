# India School and Teacher Platforms

| Platform | Link | Useful For |
|---|---|---|
| DIKSHA | https://diksha.gov.in/ | NCERT, CBSE, NIOS and State/UT school content, textbooks, videos, interactives, courses, quizzes, teacher-development resources, and offline-access options |
| NCERT on DIKSHA | https://diksha.gov.in/ncert/ | NCERT e-resources, student/teacher content, documents, video, audio, interactives, and courses |
| ePathshala | https://epathshala.nic.in/ | NCERT textbooks and digital educational resources |
| NCERT | https://ncert.nic.in/ | Curriculum frameworks, textbooks, publications, teacher resources, and national school-education materials |
| SWAYAM | https://swayam.gov.in/ | Free online courses from Indian institutions across school, higher education, engineering, science, humanities, management, and more; certification may involve an exam/fee |
| NPTEL | https://www.nptel.ac.in/ | IIT/IISc courses in engineering, science, mathematics, computing, humanities, and professional topics; learning content is free |
| National Digital Library of India | https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in/ | Search and discovery across books, articles, theses, learning resources, and educational collections |
| Virtual Labs | https://www.vlab.co.in/ | Online laboratory experiences across engineering and science disciplines |
| SWAYAM PRABHA | https://www.swayamprabha.gov.in/ | Educational DTH channels and scheduled learning content |

## For School Teachers

Start with DIKSHA and ePathshala when you need curriculum-linked Indian school content, then use the subject-specific international tools where they add exploration, simulation, or research depth.
