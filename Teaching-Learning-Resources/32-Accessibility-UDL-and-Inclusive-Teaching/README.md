## Inclusive Design Questions

- Can learners access the information in more than one way?
- Can they show understanding in more than one appropriate way?
- Are language, colour, sound, physical movement or device requirements creating unnecessary barriers?
- Is the challenge in the learning goal, or accidentally in the format?
