# Research Libraries and Academic Search

A practical index for finding research papers, reviews, education studies, preprints, and open-access scholarly material.

> **Tip:** A search result is not automatically trustworthy. Check author, publication, date, evidence, peer-review status, and whether the full text actually supports the claim you want to use.

| Resource | Link | Useful For | Access Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Scholar | https://scholar.google.com/ | Broad scholarly search across articles, theses, books, conference papers, citations, and repositories | Search is free; full-text access depends on the source |
| Semantic Scholar | https://www.semanticscholar.org/ | Finding research papers, related work, citations, and topic connections | Free research discovery; full text varies |
| CORE | https://core.ac.uk/ | Searching a very large collection of open-access research outputs from repositories and journals | Strong choice when you specifically want open-access material |
| DOAJ | https://doaj.org/ | Finding peer-reviewed open-access journals and articles across disciplines | Open-access journal directory |
| ERIC | https://eric.ed.gov/ | Education research, teaching methods, curriculum, assessment, classroom studies, and policy | Free search; many records include full text |
| arXiv | https://arxiv.org/ | Recent research and preprints in mathematics, physics, computer science, statistics, engineering, and related fields | Free; remember that preprints are not automatically peer reviewed |
| PubMed | https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ | Biomedical, health, life-science, and medical research literature | Search is free; full text varies |
| PubMed Central | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ | Free full-text biomedical and life-science research articles | Free full text |
| Directory of Open Access Books | https://www.doabooks.org/ | Peer-reviewed open-access academic books | Open access |
| Crossref Metadata Search | https://search.crossref.org/ | Checking article titles, authors, DOIs, publication details, and citation metadata | Free metadata search |

## Teacher Use

Useful for:

- literature reviews,
- evidence for lesson design,
- education-research assignments,
- STEM competition background research,
- checking AI-generated claims,
- finding original research behind popular science reports.

## Student Research Rule

```text
Search -> Open Source -> Read -> Compare -> Cite -> Explain
```

Do not cite a search-results page as evidence. Open and read the actual source.
