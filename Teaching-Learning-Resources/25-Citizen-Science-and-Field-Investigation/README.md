# Citizen Science and Field Investigation

| Resource | Link | Useful For | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| GLOBE Program | https://www.globe.gov/ | School field protocols, atmosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere, soil, environmental measurements | Many materials free; some participation requires account/training |
| GLOBE Observer | https://observer.globe.gov/ | Citizen science observations for clouds, land cover, trees and more | Free app; account/age rules apply |
| SciStarter Education | https://pages.scistarter.org/education/ | Citizen-science projects selected for education and grade levels | Free discovery; project rules vary |
| Zooniverse Education | https://www.zooniverse.org/get-involved/educate | Authentic research classification projects and educator resources | Free; account/age requirements vary |
| iNaturalist | https://www.inaturalist.org/ | Biodiversity observation, species documentation, local ecology | Free; account/privacy/age rules apply |
| eBird | https://ebird.org/ | Bird observation, biodiversity, migration, local field data | Free; account required for contribution |
| NASA GLOBE Observer connections | https://observer.globe.gov/ | Earth observations tied to NASA science | Free |
| CoCoRaHS | https://www.cocorahs.org/ | Community rain, hail and snow measurements | Participation rules vary by location |
| Project FeederWatch | https://feederwatch.org/ | Bird observations and seasonal data | Participation/access conditions may vary |
| Globe at Night | https://globeatnight.org/ | Measuring night-sky brightness and light pollution | Free participation |
| Foldit | https://fold.it/ | Citizen science through protein-folding puzzles | Free; account/software conditions apply |

## Field Investigation Rule

For students, check supervision, location safety, privacy, permissions, age requirements, and whether uploading geolocation or images is appropriate.
