## Existing Curriculum Materials

The working curriculum materials also include the STREAM.AI curriculum framework and school proposal used for grade progression, learning evidence, assessment, robotics, AI/data literacy, IoT, and design-thinking planning.

## Important Distinction

Detailed school-specific lesson plans, bootcamp packs, worksheets, capstone briefs, and final rubrics should be developed and reviewed for the actual timetable, students, equipment, and school policy rather than being presented as universally finished resources.
