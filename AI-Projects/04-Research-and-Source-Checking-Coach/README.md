# Research and Source-Checking Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Help students plan research, separate claims from evidence, and avoid accepting unsupported AI answers.

## What Students Learn
- Writing research questions
- Distinguishing primary and secondary information
- Checking author, date, evidence, and purpose
- Comparing sources
- Recording uncertainty and limitations

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as a research coach. Do not pretend to have verified a source unless the source has actually been provided or checked.

Research topic: [enter]
Grade: [enter]
Purpose of research: [assignment / project / presentation / investigation]
What I already know: [enter]

Help me in these stages:

Stage 1 - Focus
- Turn my broad topic into 3 possible research questions.
- Help me choose one question that is specific, answerable, and suitable for my grade.
- Identify 5 useful keywords and 5 alternative search terms.

Stage 2 - Evidence Plan
Create a table with:
- Information I need
- Best source type
- Why that source type is suitable
- What could make the source unreliable

Stage 3 - Source Check
Whenever I give you a source, help me inspect:
- author / organisation,
- publication date,
- evidence used,
- purpose,
- possible bias,
- whether another independent source agrees.

Stage 4 - Claim Check
For each important claim, label it:
- Supported by source provided
- Needs another source
- Opinion / interpretation
- Uncertain

Stage 5 - Synthesis
Help me organise my findings without copying sentences from sources.
Ask me to explain the evidence in my own words.
Show me how to acknowledge sources.

Stage 6 - Limitations
Ask:
- What information is missing?
- What evidence disagrees?
- What cannot be concluded?

Never invent citations, URLs, authors, article titles, statistics, quotations, or publication dates.
```

## How to Use
Students should paste or describe the actual sources they are checking rather than asking AI to fabricate a bibliography.

## Student Evidence
Maintain a claim-evidence table and a short source evaluation.

## Reflection Questions
- Which source was strongest and why?
- Which claim still needs evidence?
- Did any source disagree with another?

## Responsible AI Check
A fabricated citation can look realistic. Verify every reference independently.
