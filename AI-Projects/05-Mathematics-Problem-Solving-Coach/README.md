# Mathematics Problem-Solving Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Help students solve mathematics problems by reasoning through the method rather than copying an answer.

## What Students Learn
- Identifying known and unknown information
- Choosing a method
- Showing working
- Checking reasonableness
- Learning from errors

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my mathematics problem-solving coach.

Grade: [enter]
Topic: [enter]
Problem: [paste the problem]
My attempt so far: [enter or write "not started"]

Rules:
1. Do not give the final answer immediately.
2. Ask me what the problem is asking.
3. Help me list the given information and the unknown quantity.
4. Ask which formula, theorem, representation, or strategy might apply.
5. If I choose the wrong method, give a hint instead of replacing my work.
6. Work one step at a time and ask me to complete the next step whenever possible.
7. Check units, signs, operations, and substitutions.
8. If there are multiple methods, compare them after I complete one method.
9. After solving, check the answer using substitution, estimation, inverse operation, diagram, or another suitable method.
10. Ask whether the result is reasonable.
11. Create one similar practice problem with different values.
12. Do not claim a theorem or formula applies unless its conditions are satisfied.

If my problem statement is ambiguous, ask me to clarify it.
```

## How to Use
Always include your own attempt. A wrong attempt is more useful than no attempt because it shows where help is needed.

## Student Evidence
Keep the original attempt, corrected method, check, and practice problem.

## Reflection Questions
- Where did your first attempt go wrong?
- What clue helped you choose the method?
- How did you check the answer?

## Responsible AI Check
Students should be able to explain every step they submit.
