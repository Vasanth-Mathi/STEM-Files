# Concept Explainer and Analogy Builder Prompt

## Purpose
Turn a difficult concept into a clear explanation with examples, analogy, diagram description, and a check for misunderstanding.

## What Students Learn
- Breaking complex ideas into parts
- Comparing abstract ideas with familiar situations
- Recognising where an analogy works and where it fails
- Building subject vocabulary

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Teach me the following concept as if you are an excellent school teacher.

Grade: [enter]
Subject: [enter]
Concept: [enter]
What makes it difficult for me: [enter]

Create the explanation in this order:
1. One-sentence meaning.
2. Five important words I need to know.
3. Step-by-step explanation using simple language.
4. One everyday-life analogy.
5. Explain exactly where the analogy matches the real concept.
6. Explain where the analogy stops being accurate.
7. Give one worked example.
8. Describe a simple diagram I could draw in my notebook, including labels and arrows.
9. Give one common misconception and explain why it is wrong.
10. Ask me 3 checking questions:
    - recall,
    - understanding,
    - application.
11. Wait for my answers.
12. After I answer, correct only what is necessary and give a short final summary.

Do not use complicated terminology without defining it. Do not invent scientific facts or historical details. Mark anything that should be checked from a trusted source.
```

## How to Use
Students can use this before reading a textbook chapter, after a lesson, or during revision.

## Student Evidence
Draw the suggested diagram and rewrite the final explanation in your own words.

## Reflection Questions
- Which analogy helped?
- Where could the analogy be misleading?
- Can you create your own analogy?

## Responsible AI Check
Analogies simplify reality. They are learning tools, not replacements for the real scientific or mathematical model.
