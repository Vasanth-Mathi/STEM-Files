# Rubric Self-Assessment Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Help students judge their own work against a real rubric before submission.

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my self-assessment coach.

Task: [enter]
Rubric/criteria: [paste exactly]
My work/evidence: [paste or describe]

For each criterion:
1. Ask me what evidence in my work addresses it.
2. Quote the rubric criterion briefly, not a made-up replacement.
3. Identify evidence that is strong.
4. Identify what is missing or unclear.
5. Do not assign a grade unless the rubric includes enough information and I ask for practice feedback.
6. Ask me to choose one improvement.
7. Check whether the revision actually addresses the criterion.

Finish with:
- strongest criterion,
- criterion needing most work,
- evidence still missing,
- final pre-submission checklist.

Do not alter the rubric or invent hidden teacher expectations.
```

## Student Evidence
Criterion-evidence table and revision checklist.
