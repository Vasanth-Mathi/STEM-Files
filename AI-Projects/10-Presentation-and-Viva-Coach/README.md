# Presentation and Viva Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Help students prepare an evidence-based project presentation and practise answering questions.

## What Students Learn
- Organising a technical story
- Explaining decisions
- Presenting evidence
- Acknowledging limitations
- Answering viva questions clearly

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my project presentation and viva coach.

Grade: [enter]
Project title: [enter]
Problem / purpose: [enter]
What we built or investigated: [enter]
Important evidence: [test results / observations / data]
What failed or changed: [enter]
My individual contribution: [enter]

First help me build a short presentation with:
1. Problem / need
2. Users or context
3. Goal and success criteria
4. Idea / design
5. How it works
6. Important circuit, code, model, or process
7. Testing evidence
8. Failure and improvement
9. Final result
10. Limitations
11. What I learned
12. Sources / acknowledgements

For every slide:
- give a short title,
- suggest 3-5 points maximum,
- suggest one useful visual or evidence item,
- tell me what I should explain aloud rather than put on the slide.

Then conduct a viva:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Include questions about why, how, testing, limitations, safety, alternatives, and my personal contribution.
- If my answer is vague, ask a follow-up.
- Do not answer the question for me until I attempt it.
- Give feedback on accuracy, clarity, evidence, and confidence.

Do not encourage me to hide failures or exaggerate results.
```

## How to Use
Use actual project evidence. Do not invent test results just to make slides look complete.

## Student Evidence
Presentation outline, rehearsed answers, and a list of questions that exposed weak understanding.

## Reflection Questions
- Which question was hardest?
- Which claim needed better evidence?
- What can you explain confidently without notes?

## Responsible AI Check
The presenter is responsible for the accuracy of every claim shown.
