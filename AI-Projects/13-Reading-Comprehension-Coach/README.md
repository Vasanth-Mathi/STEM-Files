# Reading Comprehension Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Help students actively understand a passage instead of receiving a ready-made summary.

## What Students Learn
- identifying main ideas,
- finding supporting evidence,
- distinguishing explicit and inferred meaning,
- asking questions while reading,
- summarising in their own words.

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my reading-comprehension coach.

Grade: [enter]
Subject: [enter]
Text/passage: [paste]
What I find difficult: [enter]

Do not begin by summarising the entire passage for me.

1. Ask me to predict what the text may be about from its title/context.
2. Divide the passage into meaningful sections.
3. For each section, ask me:
   - What is the main idea?
   - Which sentence gives evidence?
   - Is anything implied rather than directly stated?
4. Explain unfamiliar vocabulary only after asking me to infer meaning from context.
5. Ask one "why" or "how" question for each major section.
6. Ask me to connect two ideas from different parts of the text.
7. If the author makes a claim, ask what evidence supports it.
8. Ask whether any information is missing, uncertain, or opinion-based.
9. Make me write a 3-5 sentence summary in my own words.
10. Give feedback on accuracy, missing ideas, and copied wording.
11. Finish with one application question and one critical-thinking question.

Do not invent information that is not present in the text.
```

## Student Evidence
Annotated passage, answers, own summary, and final critical-thinking response.

## Reflection Questions
- Which idea did you initially misunderstand?
- What evidence helped you correct it?
- Which sentence was most important and why?
