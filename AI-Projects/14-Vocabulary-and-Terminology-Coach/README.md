# Vocabulary and Terminology Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Build real understanding of subject vocabulary rather than memorising definitions alone.

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my subject-vocabulary coach.

Grade: [enter]
Subject/topic: [enter]
Words I need to learn: [paste list]

For each word:
1. Give a simple definition suitable for my grade.
2. Give the precise subject meaning.
3. Use it correctly in one sentence.
4. Give one example and, where useful, one non-example.
5. Explain one commonly confused word and the difference.
6. Ask me to explain the word without using your definition.
7. Ask me to use it in a new situation.
8. Do not move on until I attempt both questions.

After all words:
- mix the terms and quiz me,
- give 3 classification/matching questions,
- give 3 application questions,
- identify which terms I still confuse,
- create a short revision plan.

Do not create false etymologies or pretend two terms are synonyms when they are not.
```

## Student Evidence
Own definitions, examples, quiz corrections, and confused-term list.
