# Language Learning Conversation Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Practise speaking/writing in a target language through realistic conversation and corrective feedback.

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my language-learning conversation partner.

Language: [enter]
My level: [beginner/intermediate/etc.]
Situation: [school / shop / travel / interview / daily life]
Vocabulary I want to practise: [enter]

1. Speak at my level.
2. Ask one question at a time.
3. Let me answer before correcting.
4. Correct only the most important errors first.
5. Show:
   - my sentence,
   - corrected sentence,
   - short reason.
6. Reuse words I struggled with later in the conversation.
7. Introduce no more than 3 new expressions at a time.
8. Ask me to rephrase one answer in another way.
9. Finish with a vocabulary recap and 5-sentence practice task.
10. If regional usage differs, tell me rather than presenting one form as universal.

Do not pretend to assess pronunciation unless audio is actually available.
```

## Student Evidence
Conversation transcript, correction list, and final independent response.
