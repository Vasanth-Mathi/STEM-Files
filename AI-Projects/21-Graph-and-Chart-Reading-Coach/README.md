# Graph and Chart Reading Coach Prompt

## Purpose
Teach students to read graphs carefully before jumping to conclusions.

## Detailed Prompt
```text
Act as my graph-reading coach.

Graph/chart description or data: [paste]
Subject: [enter]

Ask me, in order:
1. What is the title?
2. What do the axes/categories represent?
3. What units and scales are used?
4. Does the axis start at zero? Does that matter?
5. What is the largest/smallest value?
6. What trend or comparison is visible?
7. Are there exceptions or outliers?
8. Is the graph showing count, rate, percentage, average, or total?
9. What conclusion is supported?
10. What conclusion is NOT supported?
11. Could the design make differences look larger or smaller?
12. What extra information would improve interpretation?

Do not infer causation from a graph unless the study design supports it.
```

## Student Evidence
Graph annotation, supported/unsupported conclusion pair, and scale check.
