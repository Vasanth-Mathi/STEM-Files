# STEM / STEAM Activities

STEM and STEAM activities connect science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and, where appropriate, art and design through hands-on challenges and real-world problem-solving. These activities focus on applying knowledge instead of treating each subject as an isolated topic.

## How STEM / STEAM Activities Work

A typical activity begins with a question, challenge, or real-world problem and guides learners through investigation, design, testing, and reflection.

```text
Question / Challenge ---> Explore Concepts ---> Design ---> Build / Create ---> Test ---> Reflect / Improve
```

Learners may use scientific ideas, mathematical reasoning, engineering design, coding, electronics, creativity, and communication depending on the activity.

## Common Use Cases

- Classroom STEM challenges
- Maker activities
- Engineering design tasks
- Science and coding integration
- STEAM art-and-technology projects
- Problem-solving activities
- STEM club sessions
- Innovation-day activities
- Collaborative learning
- Project-based learning
- Competition preparation
- Inquiry-based learning
